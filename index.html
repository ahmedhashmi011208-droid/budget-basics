Module 01

Budgeting Basics.

A budget is simply a plan for how money can be allocated between requirements, wants and savings.

Income

Money received from allowances, scholarships, internships, part-time work or other sources.

Fixed Expenses

Costs that generally remain similar each month, such as regular transport or fees.

Variable Expenses

Spending that can change from month to month, such as food, shopping or entertainment.

Requirements

Things that are important for daily life, education, health, transportation and similar needs.

Wants

Optional purchases that can often be delayed when money is needed elsewhere.

Savings

Money intentionally kept aside for future goals, emergencies or planned purchases.

Module 02

Needs vs Wants.

Learning to distinguish essential spending from optional spending can help students make more deliberate decisions.

Needs

Items or services that are important for everyday living, education or essential responsibilities.

  • Basic transportation
  • Educational materials
  • Essential food
  • Required bills

Wants

Optional purchases that may improve comfort or enjoyment but can often be delayed.

  • Extra shopping
  • Entertainment
  • Non-essential upgrades
  • Impulse purchases

Is it essential now?

A simple question can help you delay non-essential purchases.

YES — Prioritize If it is a need, plan for it in your budget first.
NO — Can Wait If it is a want, delay it and review after some time.

Module 06

Common money mistakes.

Explore realistic student scenarios and simple corrective actions.

01

Impulse Buying

Seeing a limited-time discount can make a purchase feel urgent.

Corrective action: pause and ask whether the item was already part of your plan.
02

Ignoring Small Expenses

Small daily purchases can add up over time.

Corrective action: include small recurring purchases in your expense plan.
03

Unused Subscriptions

Paying for a service that is rarely used can reduce money available for other goals.

Corrective action: regularly review recurring expenses.
04

Spending Without a Plan

Spending without knowing available money can make it difficult to track priorities.

Corrective action: create a simple spending plan before the month begins.
05

Delaying Important Payments

Forgetting planned payments can disturb the rest of a monthly spending plan.

Corrective action: list important due dates in advance.
06

Not Setting a Savings Goal

Without a defined goal, saving can feel less structured.

Corrective action: define a target and a simple contribution plan.

Privacy

Privacy note.

How BudgetBasics handles demonstration data and protects learner privacy.

Your data stays with you.

BudgetBasics is a front-end educational project without a server-side database. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere.

1. No server-side storage

The website does not include a backend database, login system or transaction processing. All demonstration values remain inside your browser session.

2. What stays on your device

  • Theme preference is stored in your browser’s localStorage.
  • Visit counter is stored locally in your browser.
  • Expense planner entries exist only during the current session.

3. What is not collected

  • No bank account numbers, card numbers or payment details.
  • No personal identity documents or official records.
  • No analytics that identifies individual users.

4. Forms

The Feedback form performs client-side validation only. It does not submit, store or transmit personal information.

If you would like to clear all local data, you can clear your browser storage at any time. BudgetBasics will not retain any copy.
